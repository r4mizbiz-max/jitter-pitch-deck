03Nurture · Show-Up Protection

Booked isn't the goal. Seated is.

A booked appointment means nothing if nobody's home. So we nurture every lead right up to the moment you knock.

2 Days Out

Reminder text

A friendly confirmation text locks the appointment into their week.

1 Day Out

Reminder text

A second text the day before keeps it top of mind — no surprises.

Morning Of

Live confirmation call

We call to confirm they'll be home — and that both decision-makers will be present.

If Not Both

We reschedule

If both aren't available, we move it rather than waste your trip.

Show-ups aren't guaranteed — people are people — but this protocol is why ours seat at the rate they do. (We'll explain the guarantee on the call.)
